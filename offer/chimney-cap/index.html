Straight answers for homeowners in Dallas–Fort Worth.

Do I really need a chimney cap?
A good cap helps keep out rain, animals, nesting materials, and debris — all common causes of damage, odors, and blockages.
Can you repair my cap or do I need a new one?
Sometimes repair is enough. If the cap is rusted through, missing, or poorly fitted, replacement is usually smarter. We’ll recommend honestly.
Is the estimate free?
Yes — no-obligation estimate for cap repair or replacement.
Will a cap stop animals?
Proper mesh/spark-arrestor style caps are designed to block birds, squirrels, and similar pests while allowing exhaust to exit.
