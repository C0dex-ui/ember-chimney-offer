How long does an inspection take?
Most residential inspections take about 30–60 minutes depending on access and system complexity. We’ll confirm a window when we book.
Do I need an inspection every year?
Industry guidance is to inspect chimneys annually and clean as needed. Buyers, sellers, and heavy wood burners often need more frequent checks.
